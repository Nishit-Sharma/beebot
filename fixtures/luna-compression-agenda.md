# Beginner Houseplant Workshop — 30 Minutes

**Audience:** Beginners  
**Format:** Short teaching segments, a make-believe plant activity, and questions at the end.

| Time | Section | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–3:00 | Welcome (3 min) | Welcome participants, introduce the session, and set the expectation that the goal is to learn a few simple care basics. |
| 3:00–11:00 | Light (8 min) | Explain that light needs vary by plant; discuss observing a spot’s brightness and matching a plant to its conditions. |
| 11:00–19:00 | Watering (8 min) | Cover checking soil before watering, watering thoroughly when needed, and avoiding a pot that stays waterlogged. |
| 19:00–25:00 | Imaginary plant activity (6 min) | Invite each participant to invent a houseplant: give it a name, imagine where it would live, and describe how they would decide when to water it. Emphasize that this is a pretend exercise, not a plant purchase or care demonstration. |
| 25:00–30:00 | Questions (5 min) | Reserve the closing five minutes for participant questions and brief answers. |

**Timing check:** 3 + 8 + 8 + 6 + 5 = 30 minutes. The original 45-minute timings are revised to fit the requested 30-minute total; all requested sections are retained and questions stay last.
